Software OpenGL for Pulsaar on Windows

Put opengl32.dll and libgallium_wgl.dll in the same folder as pulsaar.exe.
They are Mesa's llvmpipe renderer, built by the mesa-dist-win project
(https://github.com/pal1000/mesa-dist-win), and draw the window on the
CPU. Use them only on a machine without a GPU, such as a virtual desktop,
where Pulsaar otherwise reports that OpenGL 2.0 is missing. On a machine
with a GPU they replace the driver's OpenGL and make drawing slower.
